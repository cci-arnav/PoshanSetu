The Challenge

A nutritious meal is often a child's first reason to come to school — education is the reason they stay.

Yet millions of children face a fractured nutrition ecosystem where systemic gaps undermine both health and learning outcomes: uneven food quality, food safety concerns, poor transparency, weak local supplier networks, and limited coordination between programmes.

India already has the building blocks to fix this — mid-day meal infrastructure, a growing cloud-kitchen ecosystem, FSSAI's food safety framework, and grassroots community organisations. What's missing is a unified platform connecting them all. That's what PoshanSetu builds.

Quality & Safety Gaps

Food quality varies across schools, and safety risks threaten both student health and trust in the programme.

Weak Local Networks

Limited connections between local suppliers and kitchens mean fresher, fairer sourcing rarely reaches classrooms.

Fragmented Coordination

Poor transparency and minimal community participation leave schools and parents disconnected from meals served.

Quick FAQ

Common questions

A few things people usually ask us before getting involved.

Who runs PoshanSetu?

PoshanSetu is an independent platform that partners with schools, kitchens, SHGs, and NGOs to support the delivery of safe, quality school meals. We don't run any government meal scheme ourselves.

How does a kitchen get verified on the platform?

Every kitchen goes through a rigorous onboarding and certification process — including FSSAI license verification, hygiene and SOP training, and compliance checks — before it can accept school orders.

How can my company fulfil its CSR obligations through this programme?

We work with corporate partners to sponsor specific schools or districts, with transparent reporting on meals served and outcomes. Contact us to discuss scope and documentation.

How is meal quality monitored?

School Management Committees log meal quality and student feedback after each meal, compliance status is tracked on live dashboards, and our field teams review flagged issues on a rapid escalation pathway.
